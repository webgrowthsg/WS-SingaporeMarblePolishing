## Rules

1. No claim may be published as factual without an APPROVED status in this register.
2. BLOCKED claims are omitted from the public site or replaced with neutral, honest copy.
3. Evidence URLs are restricted — not for public display.
4. Approvals expire and must be re-verified if the underlying evidence changes.
5. Bracketed TODOs and fake contact details must never appear on the public site.
